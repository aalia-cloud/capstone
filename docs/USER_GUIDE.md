# BeeSure User Guide

## Welcome to BeeSure 🍯

BeeSure is a revolutionary honey marketplace that uses blockchain technology to ensure the authenticity and traceability of every honey product. This guide will help you navigate the platform and make the most of its features.

## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. **Visit BeeSure**: Navigate to [https://beesure.com](https://beesure.com)
2. **Sign Up**: Click "Sign Up" in the top right corner
3. **Fill Details**: Enter your name, email, and create a secure password
4. **Verify Email**: Check your email and click the verification link
5. **Complete Profile**: Add additional information like shipping address

### Connecting Your Wallet

To purchase NFT certificates and interact with blockchain features:

1. **Install MetaMask**: Download the MetaMask browser extension
2. **Create Wallet**: Set up your MetaMask wallet with a secure seed phrase
3. **Connect to BeeSure**: Click "Connect Wallet" on the BeeSure homepage
4. **Approve Connection**: Confirm the connection in MetaMask
5. **Switch Network**: Ensure you're on the correct blockchain network (Polygon recommended)

## Making Purchases

### Adding to Cart

1. **Select Product**: Choose your desired honey product
2. **Choose Quantity**: Select the number of jars
3. **Add to Cart**: Click "Add to Cart" button
4. **View Cart**: Click the cart icon to review items

### Checkout Process

1. **Review Cart**: Verify products and quantities
2. **Shipping Information**: Enter or confirm delivery address
3. **Payment Method**: Choose from available payment options:
   - Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express)
   - PayPal
   - Cryptocurrency (ETH, MATIC)
4. **NFT Certificate**: Choose to mint an NFT certificate (optional)
5. **Place Order**: Review and confirm your purchase

### NFT Certificates

BeeSure offers blockchain certificates for authenticity:

- **What is it?**: A digital certificate stored on the blockchain
- **Benefits**: Immutable proof of authenticity and origin
- **Cost**: Small additional fee for minting
- **Ownership**: Stored in your connected wallet
- **Transferable**: Can be sold or transferred to others

## Product Verification

### Barcode Scanning

Every BeeSure product includes a unique barcode for verification:

1. **Locate Barcode**: Find the QR code on product packaging
2. **Open Scanner**: Use the "Scan Barcode" feature in the app
3. **Scan Code**: Point your camera at the barcode
4. **View Results**: See product details and authenticity confirmation

### Verification Information

Successful scans display:
- Product name and details
- Origin apiary information
- Harvest date and batch number
- Quality test results
- Blockchain certificate hash
- Authenticity status

## Troubleshooting

### Technical Issues

**Website loading slowly?**
- Check your internet connection
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Try a different browser
- Disable browser extensions temporarily

**Payment failed?**
- Verify card details are correct
- Check if card has sufficient funds
- Try a different payment method
- Contact your bank if issues persist

**NFT not appearing in wallet?**
- Wait for blockchain confirmation (can take several minutes)
- Refresh your wallet
- Check you're on the correct network
- Add the contract address manually if needed
